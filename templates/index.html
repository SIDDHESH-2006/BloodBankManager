Relational Architecture & Third Normal Form (3NF) Normalization

As detailed in Slide 7 of Review 2 presentation.

1NF (First Normal Form)

Each field stores one atomic value. No repeating groups or comma-separated lists exist in any column. Blood units, donations, and quality checks are separated into discrete rows.

2NF (Second Normal Form)

Every non-key attribute is fully functionally dependent on the primary key. In the COMPATIBILITY table with composite key (recipient_group, donor_group), there are no partial dependencies.

3NF (Third Normal Form)

Non-key attributes depend only on the candidate key and nothing else (no transitive dependencies). Donor phone is in DONOR, not duplicated inside DONATION or BLOOD_UNIT.

Relational Tables & Constraints (Slide 3)

DONOR
  • PK: donor_id
  • full_name, phone
  • blood_group (CHECK ABO/Rh)
  • last_donated (Updated by trigger)
DONATION
  • PK: donation_id
  • FK: donor_id
  • donated_on, volume_ml (> 0)
  • hb_level (CHECK ≥ 12.0)
BLOOD_UNIT
  • PK: unit_id
  • FK: donation_id
  • blood_group, expiry_date
  • status (CHECK AVAILABLE, etc.)
BLOOD_QUALITY_CHECK
  • PK: check_id
  • FK: unit_id
  • quality_status (PASSED/FAILED)
  • action_taken (RELEASED/HOLD)
HOSPITAL
  • PK: hospital_id
  • name, city, phone
BLOOD_REQUEST
  • PK: request_id
  • FK: hospital_id
  • blood_group, units_needed
  • urgency (CRITICAL, URGENT, NORMAL)
ALLOCATION
  • PK: alloc_id
  • FK: request_id
  • FK: unit_id (UNIQUE)
  • allocated_on (TIMESTAMP)
COMPATIBILITY
  • PK: recipient_group
  • PK: donor_group
  • (Composite Key, ABO/Rh rules)
